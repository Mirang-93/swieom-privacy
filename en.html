Take Your Time — Privacy Policy

Take Your Time (Korean name 쉬엄쉬엄; an app for Android phones and iPhone, Android package and iOS bundle ID com.mirang.swieom, "the app") is made by Kim Junho (Mirang) ("we"), based in the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the app and we handle.

Other languages: 한국어 · 日本語 · 繁體中文 · Français

At a glance

  • There are no accounts, and we run no server that collects your information.
  • Your game records are saved on your phone. They may also be included in your phone's backup: Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone.
  • Settings and status such as your daily free rounds and ad counts are stored only on your phone. They are not backed up or sent to us.
  • The app shows Google ads, and Google collects information such as your advertising ID directly. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears first. On iPhone, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether to allow tracking; Google gets the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it.
  • This version of the app has no payments (in-app purchases). It doesn't collect payment or order information.
  • The only personal information we receive is what you send us by email.

2. How long we keep it

  • Emails: deleted within 3 months after we reply. As the app has no payments, we keep no transaction records.
  • Information on your phone: until you delete the app or use About → Delete records. "Delete records" erases game records (attendance, rounds, best scores, solved puzzles, today's play). It does not erase the install date, today's free-round count, ad counts (continue and one more round), a round you've earned by watching an ad, or whether you've seen the how-to-play guide. Deleting the app erases everything on the phone.
  • Backups. Android: if your phone's Google backup is on, one game-records file may be backed up to your own Google account and moved to a new phone; settings and status such as free-round and ad counts are never backed up. iPhone: if iCloud Backup is on or you back up your iPhone to a computer, the game-records file is included in that backup and may move to a new iPhone; the app marks the file holding settings and status such as free-round and ad counts as excluded from backup, but values Google's ads SDK stores on the iPhone (such as your ad consent choice and SDK settings) may be included. Google and Apple manage backups under your account and device settings. We can't see your backup, and we can't promise your records will come back after reinstalling.

3. Sharing

We don't sell or share the information we receive, except where the law requires it. (Separately, Google's ad SDK collects data directly, as described in section 4.) We use NAVER Corporation (Republic of Korea) to receive and store emails.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  1. The app includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). You see an ad when the app starts, when you choose to watch an ad to continue (up to 3 times a day), and when you tap "Watch an ad for one more round" after using today's 10 free Choose & Play rounds (up to 5 times a day). Ads to continue or for one more round appear only when you tap the button. There are no banner or interstitial ads.
  2. The SDK sends Google device identifiers (on Android, your advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and developer-scoped identifiers such as the IDFV), IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), app interactions (app launches, taps, ad video views), diagnostics and, where applicable, your ad consent choice. Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if allowed, personalize ads. How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites On iPhone, the app asks with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before showing ads (after Google's consent form where that appears); every feature works whether or not you allow tracking. Ad performance may also be measured through Apple's SKAdNetwork.
  3. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires consent, Google's consent form appears when you open the app. You can use the app even if you don't consent; Google may then show non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with About → Ad privacy settings. This button appears only where consent is required.
  4. Your choices: on Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by phone) and turn off Android ad privacy features in the same menu where supported; on iPhone, turn off tracking for this app in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; manage personalized ads at https://myadcenter.google.com; use About → Ad privacy settings where shown; or delete the app.

5. Payment

This version of the app has no payments (in-app purchases). The app doesn't ask Google Play or the App Store about purchases or your billing country, and we don't receive or keep payment or order information. If an earlier version with payments stored a purchase status, billing country or pre-purchase consent record on your phone, those values may stay on your phone only; they are not sent to us or backed up, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold, or to stop using it, by emailing wnshroqkf@naver.com. We'll reply without undue delay. Information on your phone you can delete yourself (section 2). For information Google collects, contact Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect children's personal information; if we learn we have, we delete it.

8. Security

We run no server and handle only the minimum. App data is kept in storage that Android or iOS lets only this app read. Our email, Google Play Console and App Store Connect accounts are protected by two-step verification. Google says AdMob data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

9. Regional information

United States. We don't sell personal information. Google's ad SDK collects data directly as described in section 4. The app has no web browsing, so it receives no browser "Do Not Track" signals and has nothing to respond to. Depending on your state you may have additional privacy rights; email us and we'll help.

EEA, UK and Switzerland. We are the controller for the information in section 1.1. Legal bases: replying to you — our legitimate interest in handling requests; ads — the consent you give in Google's consent form. Your information may be processed in the Republic of Korea (where we are) and in the United States and other countries (Google). The European Commission has recognised the Republic of Korea as ensuring an adequate level of protection (adequacy decision of 17 December 2021); Google relies on the transfer safeguards described in its own privacy policy. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and you can withdraw ad consent at any time (About → Ad privacy settings). You may complain to your local data protection authority (in France: CNIL, www.cnil.fr).
