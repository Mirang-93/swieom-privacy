Take Your Time — Privacy Policy

Take Your Time (Korean name 쉬엄쉬엄; Android package com.mirang.swieom, "the app") is made by Mirang ("we"), based in the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the app and we handle.

Other languages: 한국어 · 日本語 · 繁體中文 · Français

At a glance - There are no accounts, and we run no server that collects your information. - Your game records are saved on your phone. If your phone's Google backup is on, they may also be backed up to your own Google account. - To show the right purchase terms, the app stores your Google Play billing country and, in places such as the EU and Taiwan, the record of your consent before payment — only on your phone. They are not sent to us. - While you use the app for free, it shows Google ads, and Google collects information such as your advertising ID directly. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears first. - Google Play handles payment. We never receive your card details. - The only personal information we receive is what you send us by email (questions, refunds) and the order information Google Play gives sellers.

1.3 Information Google collects directly — see sections 4 and 5.

2. How long we keep it

  • Emails about purchases: kept as long as Korean e-commerce law requires us to keep transaction records (contracts and cancellations 5 years, payments and delivery 5 years, complaints and disputes 3 years), then deleted. Other emails: deleted within 3 months after we reply.
  • Information on your phone: until you delete the app or use About → Delete records. "Delete records" erases game records (attendance, rounds, best scores, solved puzzles, today's play). It does not erase the install date, purchase status, ad counts, billing country or the pre-purchase consent record. Deleting the app erases everything on the phone.
  • Google backup: if your phone's Google backup is on, one game-records file may be backed up to your own Google account and moved to a new phone. Purchase status, ad counts, billing country and the consent record are never backed up. We can't see your backup, and we can't promise your records will come back after reinstalling.

3. Sharing

We don't sell or share the information we receive, except where the law requires it. We use NAVER Corporation (Republic of Korea) to receive and store emails.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  1. The app includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Free users see an ad when the app starts and when they watch an ad to continue. There are no banner or interstitial ads.
  2. The SDK sends Google your advertising ID, app set ID, IP address, app interactions (app launches, taps, ad video views), diagnostics and, where applicable, your ad consent choice. Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if allowed, personalize ads. How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  3. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires consent, Google's consent form appears when you open the app. You can use the app even if you don't consent; Google may then show non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with About → Ad privacy settings. This button appears only where consent is required and only while you haven't bought "Choose & Play + no ads".
  4. After you buy "Choose & Play + no ads", the app no longer requests or shows ads. The ads SDK stays in the app for free users and may still contact Google when the app runs.
  5. Your choices: delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by phone); turn off Android ad privacy features in the same menu where supported; manage personalized ads at https://myadcenter.google.com; use About → Ad privacy settings where shown; or delete the app.

5. Payment

Google Play processes payment; we never receive card details (see the Google Payments privacy notice). The app asks Google Play on your phone whether you've bought "Choose & Play + no ads" and keeps the answer only on your phone; on a new phone or after reinstalling, it checks again with the same Google account (Restore purchase). When you open the purchase screen, the app asks Google Play for your billing country to show the right terms. The billing country and the consent record stay on your phone; they leave it only if you press "Save or share these terms" and send them yourself.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold, or to stop using it, by emailing wnshroqkf@naver.com. We'll reply without undue delay. We may keep transaction records the law requires us to keep. Information on your phone you can delete yourself (section 2). For information Google collects, contact Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Children

The app is for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect children's personal information; if we learn we have, we delete it.

8. Security

We run no server and handle only the minimum. App data is kept in storage only this app can read. Our email and Google Play Console accounts are protected by two-step verification. Google says AdMob data is encrypted in transit (TLS).
