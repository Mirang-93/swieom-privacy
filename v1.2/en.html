Take Your Time — Privacy Policy

Take Your Time (Korean name 쉬엄쉬엄; an app for Android phones and iPhone, Android package and iOS bundle ID com.mirang.swieom, "the app") is made by Kim Junho (Mirang) ("we"), based in the Republic of Korea. This policy explains what information the app and we handle.

Other languages: 한국어 · 日本語 · 繁體中文 · Français

At a glance

  • There are no accounts, and we run no server that collects your information.
  • Your game records are saved on your phone. They may also be included in your phone's backup: Google backup on Android, iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone.
  • To show the right purchase terms, the app stores your billing country (Google Play or App Store) and, in places such as the EU and Taiwan, the record of your consent before payment — only on your phone. They are not sent to us.
  • While you use the app for free, it shows Google ads, and Google collects information such as your advertising ID directly. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form appears first. On iPhone, Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt asks whether to allow tracking; Google gets the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow it.
  • Google Play (Android) or Apple's App Store (iPhone) handles payment. We never receive your card details.
  • The only personal information we receive is what you send us by email (questions, refunds) and the order information Google Play gives sellers. For App Store purchases, Apple doesn't give us information that identifies the buyer.

1.3 Information Google and Apple collect directly — see sections 4 and 5.

2. How long we keep it

  • Emails about purchases: kept as long as Korean e-commerce law requires us to keep transaction records (contracts and cancellations 5 years, payments and delivery 5 years, complaints and disputes 3 years), then deleted. Other emails: deleted within 3 months after we reply.
  • Information on your phone: until you delete the app or use About → Delete records. "Delete records" erases game records (attendance, rounds, best scores, solved puzzles, today's play). It does not erase the install date, purchase status, ad counts (continue and one more round), a round you've earned by watching an ad, billing country or the pre-purchase consent record. Deleting the app erases everything on the phone.
  • Backups. Android: if your phone's Google backup is on, one game-records file may be backed up to your own Google account and moved to a new phone; purchase status, ad counts, billing country and the consent record are never backed up. iPhone: if iCloud Backup is on or you back up your iPhone to a computer, the game-records file is included in that backup and may move to a new iPhone; the app marks the file holding purchase status, ad counts, billing country and the consent record as excluded from backup, but values Google's ads SDK stores on the iPhone (such as your ad consent choice and SDK settings) may be included. Google and Apple manage backups under your account and device settings. We can't see your backup, and we can't promise your records will come back after reinstalling.

3. Sharing

We don't sell or share the information we receive, except where the law requires it. (Separately, Google's ad SDK collects data directly, as described in section 4, and Google or Apple handles payment, as described in section 5.) We use NAVER Corporation (Republic of Korea) to receive and store emails.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

  1. The app includes Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK). Free users see an ad when the app starts, when they choose to watch an ad to continue, and when they finish Today's play and tap "Watch an ad for one more round" (up to 5 times a day). Ads to continue or for one more round appear only when you tap the button. There are no banner or interstitial ads.
  2. The SDK sends Google device identifiers (on Android, your advertising ID and app set ID; on iPhone, the advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking, and developer-scoped identifiers such as the IDFV), IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), app interactions (app launches, taps, ad video views), diagnostics and, where applicable, your ad consent choice. Google uses this to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and, if allowed, personalize ads. How Google uses information from apps: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites On iPhone, the app asks with Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before showing ads (after Google's consent form where that appears); every feature works whether or not you allow tracking. Ad performance may also be measured through Apple's SKAdNetwork.
  3. In the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and other places where the law requires consent, Google's consent form appears when you open the app. You can use the app even if you don't consent; Google may then show non-personalized or limited ads. You can change your choice at any time with About → Ad privacy settings. This button appears only where consent is required and only while you haven't bought "Choose & Play + no ads".
  4. After you buy "Choose & Play + no ads", the app no longer requests or shows ads. The ads SDK stays in the app for free users and may still contact Google when the app runs.
  5. Your choices: on Android, delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings → Privacy → Ads (menu names vary by phone) and turn off Android ad privacy features in the same menu where supported; on iPhone, turn off tracking for this app in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; manage personalized ads at https://myadcenter.google.com; use About → Ad privacy settings where shown; or delete the app.

5. Payment

Google Play (Android app) or Apple's App Store (iPhone app) processes payment; we never receive card details (see the Google Payments privacy notice or Apple's Privacy Policy, https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). On the App Store, an Apple company sells and collects payment on our behalf and handles refunds (United States: Apple Inc.; Japan: iTunes K.K.; Korea: Apple Services Pte. Ltd.; Taiwan and Hong Kong: Apple Distribution International Ltd.). Apple doesn't give us your name, Apple Account or other details that identify you; App Store Connect shows us only sales totals by country. The app asks Google Play or the App Store on your phone whether you've bought "Choose & Play + no ads" and keeps the answer only on your phone (excluded from backups); on a new phone or after reinstalling, it checks again with the same Google account or Apple Account (Restore purchase). When you open the purchase screen, the app asks Google Play or the App Store for your billing country to show the right terms. The billing country and the consent record stay on your phone; they leave it only if you press "Save or share these terms" and send them yourself.

6. Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold, or to stop using it, by emailing wnshroqkf@naver.com. We'll reply without undue delay. We may keep transaction records the law requires us to keep. Information on your phone you can delete yourself (section 2). For information Google collects, contact Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy — for information Apple processes for payment, contact Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

7. Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We don't knowingly collect children's personal information; if we learn we have, we delete it.

8. Security

We run no server and handle only the minimum. App data is kept in storage that Android or iOS lets only this app read. Our email, Google Play Console and App Store Connect accounts are protected by two-step verification. Google says AdMob data is encrypted in transit (TLS).

9. Regional information

United States. We don't sell personal information. Google's ad SDK collects data directly as described in section 4. The app has no web browsing, so it receives no browser "Do Not Track" signals and has nothing to respond to. Depending on your state you may have additional privacy rights; email us and we'll help.

EEA, UK and Switzerland. We are the controller for the information in section 1.1. Legal bases: replying to you and handling refunds — performance of a contract and our legitimate interest in handling requests; keeping transaction records — our legitimate interest in proving transactions; ads — the consent you give in Google's consent form. Your information may be processed in the Republic of Korea (where we are) and in the United States and other countries (Google). The European Commission has recognised the Republic of Korea as ensuring an adequate level of protection (adequacy decision of 17 December 2021); Google relies on the transfer safeguards described in its own privacy policy. You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, and you can withdraw ad consent at any time (About → Ad privacy settings). You may complain to your local data protection authority (in France: CNIL, www.cnil.fr).

Japan. Japanese-language information required by the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (including information sent outside the app) is on the 日本語 page.

Taiwan and Hong Kong. Information required by Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act and Hong Kong's Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance is on the 繁體中文 page.

Korea. The Korean-language page follows Korea's Personal Information Protection Act.
